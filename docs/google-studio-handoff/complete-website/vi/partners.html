Distributor & Trade Program

Build a reliable stone program with a manufacturing partner in Vietnam.

WHITEROCK supports distributors, home-center suppliers, fabricators, builders, hospitality buyers, and project contractors with samples, drawings, repeat production, export packing, and shipment coordination.

Target Markets

Trade support for qualified regional programs.

  • United States & Canada
  • Caribbean & Latin America
  • Australia & New Zealand
  • Middle East
  • Europe and other qualified markets

Program MOQ

Typical starting quantities are 10–20 pieces per style or color; final MOQ depends on material, size, and packaging.

Lead Time

Most production programs are quoted at 25–55 days after sample, drawing, deposit, and material approval.

Samples

Color chips and selected product samples can be prepared for buyer review before volume production.

Export Support

Commercial invoices, packing lists, product labels, export crates, and loading coordination are available by order.
