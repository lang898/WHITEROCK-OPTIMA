A complete brief helps the factory quote the right path.

You can send an early idea, but the fastest quotations include drawings or dimensions, material direction, quantity, packing requirements, destination, and any inspection or document needs.

  • Product type or SKU family
  • Material, color, finish, and thickness
  • Dimensions, drawings, cutouts, and edge profile
  • Quantity, packing preference, and destination
  • Target timeline, inspection needs, and document requests
