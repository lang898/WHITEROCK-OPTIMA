Quality & Fabrication Safety

Specify clearly. Fabricate safely. Document every shipment.

Crystalline silica safety

Cutting, grinding, drilling, or polishing stone can generate respirable crystalline silica dust. Fabricators must use effective wet methods, local exhaust ventilation, approved respiratory protection, exposure controls, training, and housekeeping required by applicable law and the product SDS.
