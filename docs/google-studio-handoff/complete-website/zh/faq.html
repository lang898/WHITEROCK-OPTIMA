Buyer FAQ

Answers for samples, quotations, production, inspection, and export packing.

Common questions from wholesale, project, and distributor buyers.

Common Questions

Plan the order before production begins.

Ask a project question
What information is needed for a quotation?

Send product type, material, dimensions or drawings, finish, edge profile, cutouts, quantity, packaging requirements, destination, and target timing.

Can WHITEROCK make custom sizes and shapes?

Yes. Custom work is reviewed against drawings, material limits, tooling, tolerance, quantity, packing, and handling requirements before quotation.

How are samples approved?

The buyer confirms material, color direction, finish, thickness, and acceptable variation. Natural stone remains subject to lot variation, so final approval should use a physical sample or lot reference.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on material, product type, fabrication complexity, packing method, and production planning. Final MOQ is confirmed in the quotation, not assumed from the website.

What production lead time should buyers expect?

Lead time is confirmed after drawings, sample approval, deposit terms, material availability, and packaging requirements are reviewed. The website guidance is indicative only.

Can third-party inspection be arranged?

Yes. Buyer-appointed or recognized third-party inspection can be coordinated before shipment by agreement. Inspection scope and timing should be confirmed before production.

How are stone products packed for export?

Packing may include foam, corner protection, cartons, labeled plywood crates, pallets, or A-frames according to product weight, fragility, and shipment method.

Do you provide private-label or OEM/ODM development?

Yes. WHITEROCK can review samples, drawings, packaging, labels, and repeat production needs for qualified programs.

Which drawing files are most helpful?

Dimensioned PDFs are the safest starting point. CAD, DXF, shop drawings, sink templates, hole-center details, and marked revision numbers help reduce clarification cycles.

Can one order mix multiple SKUs, sizes, or colors?

Mixed orders can be reviewed when packing, labeling, production sequence, and container planning are clear. The quotation will confirm whether the mix is practical.

Can WHITEROCK support color or finish matching?

The team can review a physical sample, reference color, finish target, or approved swatch. Final matching must be confirmed by buyer approval before production.

Are all website product visuals real photos?

Owner-supplied photos are used where available. Any generated product, application, or equipment image is labeled as illustrative and should be replaced by real photography when available.

How do we request a sample kit?

Use the color sample builder or inquiry form with your target material, color family, project type, and shipping region. Sample availability and shipping details are confirmed before dispatch.

Can packaging use buyer labels or private marks?

Private-label packaging, carton labels, crate marks, and SKU labels can be reviewed during order setup. Final artwork and marking rules should be approved before production.

What documents can be prepared for shipment?

Commercial documents, packing lists, inspection references, and product-specific documents can be discussed at quotation stage. Certification and test-report claims are published only after owner verification.
